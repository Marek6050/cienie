## Wspólny styl (dopisz na końcu każdego promptu)

> Editorial documentary photograph, natural soft daylight, shallow depth of field, muted warm-neutral palette (off-white, sand, soft grey) with a subtle violet accent (#5b47e0) in small details only, calm and dignified mood, realistic, high detail, no text, no captions, no logos, no watermarks, no recognisable real people.

Dla grafik 02 i 05 zmień paletę na czarno-białą / stonowaną, tak jak w opisie poniżej.
Temat dotyczy deportacji i śmierci cywilów — żadnej przemocy, krwi ani efekciarstwa; powaga, nie rozrywka.

## Prompty

**01 · hero-uczniowie (4:5)**
A small group of Polish high-school students (age 15–17, diverse, casual school clothes) sitting close together in a bright classroom, all looking down at their smartphones with focused, thoughtful expressions. On one phone screen, a softly lit vintage-style historical scene (winter road, dark silhouettes) with two choice buttons is visible but unreadable. A teacher stands blurred in the background. Fresh, optimistic yet serious tone. Vertical composition, 4:5.

**02 · lekcja-tradycyjna (4:3)**
Black-and-white photograph of a teenage student in a hoodie seen from behind, slouched at a school desk, chin resting on hand, looking bored toward a teacher and a projected world map at the front of a dim classroom. Desaturated, slightly cold, low energy, empty desks around. Horizontal composition, 4:3.

**03 · lekcja-interaktywna (4:3)**
A teenage student in a bright classroom holding a smartphone with both hands, leaning slightly forward, engaged and curious. The phone screen shows a vintage sepia-toned historical street scene with a short text box and three answer options (text unreadable). Classmates blurred behind, one pointing at a screen. Warm natural light, a hint of violet in the phone UI. Horizontal composition, 4:3.

**04 · jak-dziala-klasa (4:3)**
A history teacher standing beside a small group of students in a classroom discussing choices together; a projector in the background shows a map of Upper Silesia in muted colours. Students hold phones and laugh or debate quietly. Collaborative, lively but respectful atmosphere. Horizontal composition, 4:3.

**05 · historia-okladka (16:10)**
Cinematic key art, winter 1945, Upper Silesia countryside: a snow-covered village road at dusk, a woman kneeling in the snow holding a small child, a young boy beside her holding a small porcelain angel figurine. In the far background, distant dark silhouettes of soldiers and a faint glow of fire on the horizon. Cold desaturated blue-grey palette with a single warm accent, painterly yet photographic, heavy but dignified atmosphere, no gore, no faces in close-up. Horizontal 16:10, leave the lower third visually calmer for text overlay.

**06 · szkola-nauczyciel (4:5)**
A history teacher (woman in her 40s, cardigan, kind attentive expression) leaning over a school desk helping two students with their smartphones; on the screens a vintage historical scene is visible but unreadable. Bright modern Polish classroom, plants on the windowsill, daylight. Warm, trustworthy, professional. Vertical 4:5.

**07 · instytucje-hero (4:5)**
Inside a quiet, minimalist museum gallery with pale walls and soft spotlights: two visitors (one adult, one teenager) standing in front of a glass display case with old documents and photographs, both looking at a phone screen showing an interactive historical story (text unreadable). Calm, contemplative, contemporary exhibition design. Vertical 4:5.

**08 · instytucje-muzeum (4:3)**
Close-up detail inside a museum: a visitor's hand holding a smartphone in front of a display case containing archival documents, old photographs and a map; the phone screen glows softly with a historical scene. Shallow depth of field, soft museum lighting. Horizontal 4:3.

**09 · instytucje-dom-kultury (4:3)**
A community cultural centre room: a mixed-age group of local residents (seniors and young people) sitting around a large table covered with old family photographs, letters and a laptop, listening to an elderly woman telling a story. Warm, welcoming, slightly worn-in interior with a notice board in the background. Horizontal 4:3.

**10 · instytucje-pomnik (4:3)**
Early evening at an outdoor memorial site with a large dark stone/bronze monument (abstract, no identifiable real monument): a lone visitor stands in front of it looking at a smartphone, a small QR plaque visible on a stand nearby. Overcast sky, calm and reflective, candles or flowers at the base. Respectful, dignified. Horizontal 4:3.

**11 · instytucje-zrodla (16:10)**
Top-down flat-lay on a wooden desk: archival black-and-white photographs, handwritten letters, a folded old map, a magnifying glass, a notebook with a pencil, and an open laptop showing a blurred scene-editor interface with a few cards and branching arrows (text unreadable). Soft window light from the left, tidy composition, muted tones. Horizontal 16:10.

## Uwagi

- Prawa: grafiki generowane — zachowaj informację o narzędziu i prompcie. Nie używaj grafik 05 i 10 jako „zdjęć archiwalnych”; w opisach są sceną rekonstruowaną, nie źródłem.
- Jeśli generator dodaje tekst na ekranach telefonów, wymaż go lub wygeneruj ponownie — tekst w obrazie ma być nieczytelny.
- Pliki powyżej ~300 KB warto skompresować do WebP (`cwebp -q 82 plik.png -o plik.webp`).
